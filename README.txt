OLYMPIAD CHAMP — a personal training site for a young maths olympian
====================================================================

Made for: an 11-year-old working through "Maths Olympiad — Unleash the
Maths Olympian In You!", who wants to get stronger at:
  * understanding word problems and turning the "data given" into equations
  * fast calculation (x11, x25, x9, squares ending in 5, ...)
  * divisibility rules and finding prime numbers

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
1. Unzip (if you received the zip) so you have a folder named "olympiad-champ".
2. Double-click "index.html" — it opens in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
3. That's it. No internet needed except the first load of fonts (the site
   still works fully offline; the fonts just fall back gracefully).

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
index.html            Home — greeting, daily brain warm-up, missions,
                      and stories of Ramanujan & Shakuntala Devi
word-problems.html    THE core trainer: 13 word problems in 3 levels.
                      Each one runs a timer and walks through
                      1) Understand (he types the given data + the question)
                      2) Plan (he writes his equation)
                      3) Solve (answer + check)
                      Then it shows a side-by-side "Champion's path":
                      his understanding vs the expert's, the efficient
                      solution steps, a speed tip, and how his time compares
                      with the Olympian target. Ends with a reflection note
                      ("one thing I learned") that goes to his journal.
speed-lab.html        Timed drills (10 questions each):
                      Lightning Multiplication, Divisibility Detective,
                      Prime Patrol, Mixed Sprint. Every answer shows the
                      trick used, so misses become lessons.
toolbox.html          The cheat-sheet: word-problem translator (English
                      to maths), full divisibility rules, prime-number
                      toolkit with the square-root test, calculation
                      shortcuts, and the champion's 4-step method.
dashboard.html        Progress: XP + ranks (Rookie to Maths Champion),
                      day streak, accuracy, average time, a speed-curve
                      chart, skill power bars, 10 unlockable badges,
                      the learning journal and the training log.
assets/style.css      Shared design
assets/app.js         Shared engine (progress, XP, badges, timer)

WHERE IS PROGRESS STORED?
-------------------------
In the browser's localStorage on this computer — nothing leaves the
machine, no account, no server. If he uses a different browser or
computer, progress starts fresh (the Dashboard has a reset button too).

TIPS FOR PARENTS
----------------
* 10–15 minutes a day beats 2 hours once a week — the day-streak and
  XP system reward showing up.
* Ask him to explain ONE word problem to you from the Champion's path
  screen — teaching it back is the fastest way to lock it in.
* The timer is framed as a training partner, not a threat: accuracy
  first, speed second.

Aligned with the book's chapters: Whole Numbers · Solve by Comparison
and Replacement · Write Equations · Divisibility · Speed · Ratio ·
Profit and Loss · Geometric Problems · Number Pattern · Pigeonhole
Principle.
