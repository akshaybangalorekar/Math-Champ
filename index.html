Brain warm-up of the day

Legends who were once beginners too

India · 1887–1920

Srinivasa Ramanujan

As a boy in Kumbakonam he loved numbers so much he filled notebooks with them — mostly on his own. One day in London he was ill, and his friend Hardy visited and mentioned his taxi number, 1729, seemed dull. Ramanujan sat up at once: "No! It is a very interesting number — it is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two cubes in two different ways." He saw beauty in a taxi number. Numbers hide secrets — you just need to look closer.

India · 1929–2013

Shakuntala Devi

The "Human Computer" of Bengaluru could multiply two 13-digit numbers in her head faster than a machine. Was she born with it? She said her gift came from practice and fascination, doing shows from age 6 and exploring numbers every single day. Fast calculation is trained, not inherited — exactly what the Speed Lab is for.
